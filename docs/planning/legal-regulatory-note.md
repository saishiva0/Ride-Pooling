# RidePool — Legal / Regulatory Note

> Status: Phase 0 — Planning
> ⚠️ This document is NOT legal advice. It records product assumptions and
> flags what must be reviewed by qualified counsel.

## 1. LEGAL / REGULATORY REVIEW REQUIRED

Before public commercial launch, the team requires a qualified review covering
(among others):

- Applicable motor vehicle regulations
- Aggregator requirements where applicable
- State-specific requirements
- Cost-sharing model
- Platform role
- User terms
- Privacy
- Location data
- Insurance considerations
- Consumer protection
- Tax / accounting implications

## 2. Product Assumptions (NOT legal facts)

- RidePool acts as a technology platform connecting Ride Creators and Ride
  Participants.
- Pricing is presented as cost sharing / estimated contribution, not a
  commercial fare.
- RidePool does not own vehicles, operate a fleet, or employ drivers.
- V1 has no payments and no platform commission.

These are **product assumptions** and do not constitute legal conclusions.
RidePool does **not** claim to be legally exempt because it acts as a
"middleman."

## 3. What Has NOT Been Determined

- Whether the platform, creators, or participants are subject to transport /
  aggregator regulation in any launch jurisdiction.
- Which state-specific rules apply.
- Tax treatment of contributions.
- Insurance requirements.

These are explicitly open: see OD-017 (regulatory) and OD-016 (launch city).
